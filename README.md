# MACROW Growth Engine

Build MACROW — Global Digital, Marcomm & Technology Growth Platform

Build a premium, modern, highly professional website/web application for a global services company called MACROW.

1. Brand Overview

Brand Name: MACROW

Brand Positioning:
MACROW is a global Digital, Marcomm, Technology and Growth Services company helping businesses move from zero to enterprise scale through integrated marketing, technology, creative and business solutions.

MACROW is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, but the website must be designed for a global audience and should feel like an international consulting and technology company rather than a local Indian agency.

Brand Concept

The name MACROW is inspired by the concept of a Crow / Raven.

The crow/raven represents:

Intelligence

Observation

Adaptability

Communication

Strategy

Resourcefulness

Community

Foresight

Curiosity

Problem solving

The brand should subtly use this concept throughout the experience without making the website look like a bird-themed website.

The Raven/Crow should represent the intelligence behind the business, while the different business segments represent the many capabilities of MACROW.

Do NOT overuse literal crow illustrations.

Use the raven/crow concept primarily through:

subtle visual motifs

abstract shapes

intelligent animations

geometric patterns

strategic copy

visual storytelling

occasional premium raven-inspired imagery where appropriate
